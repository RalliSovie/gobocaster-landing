Workflow

Pick. Place. Tune. Render.

Step 01

Pick a pattern.

Start with a mask direction that matches the story: architectural, organic, graphic, or abstract.

Step 02

Place the light.

Compose the projection as part of the shot, just like a motivated light or practical source.

Step 03

Tune the projection.

Adjust scale, contrast, color, softness, and rotation until the shadow shape supports the mood.

Step 04

Render the mood.

Turn a plain lighting pass into directed atmosphere for stills, animation, and look development.
